Autonomic Vitality Engine

Consumer-grade wellness estimates, not a medical device and not FDA cleared. Values are derived from camera photoplethysmography, touch pressure, and (optional) front-camera eye tracking. Do not use for diagnosis; consult a clinician for any health concern. The LF/HF ratio's validity as a direct "sympathovagal balance" index is actively debated in current HRV research — treat it as a descriptive spectral ratio, not a definitive readout of nervous system state.
